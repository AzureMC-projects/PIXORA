# Third-Party Notices

At the initial workspace stage, PIXORA does not intentionally bundle third-party fonts, icons, media assets, or codec binaries.

As dependencies and assets are introduced, this file will list names, versions, authors where required, license identifiers, and applicable notices or attribution text. Each dependency must be reviewed for compatibility with the project's Apache-2.0 license and desktop/browser distribution implications.

Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses. PIXORA's license does not replace those terms.
