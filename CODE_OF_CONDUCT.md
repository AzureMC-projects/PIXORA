# Contributor Code of Conduct

PIXORA aims to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful of people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and spaces where someone represents PIXORA. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through a verified contact channel. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation.
